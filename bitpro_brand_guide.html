Brand Overview

BitPro is a comprehensive all-in-one business management platform combining POS, ERP, Asset Management, and enterprise solutions designed for modern businesses in the Middle East and beyond.

Our Mission

To empower businesses with an integrated, intelligent platform that unifies POS, ERP, asset tracking, inventory management, and financial operations into one seamless solution.

Brand Values

Comprehensive, Integrated, Professional, Innovative, Reliable, Compliant, Enterprise-Grade, User-Focused

Target Audience

Small to large enterprises needing integrated business management from point-of-sale to back-office operations, with focus on Middle East compliance and multi-language support.

Product Suite

A complete ecosystem of integrated business management solutions.

P

POS System

Advanced point-of-sale with Arabic/English support, thermal printing, ZATCA e-invoicing compliance, and multi-payment methods.

E

ERP System

Enterprise resource planning covering sales, purchases, inventory, accounting, HR, and comprehensive financial reporting.

A

Asset Management

Complete asset tracking with device receipts, maintenance schedules, depreciation, and Excel import capabilities.

I

Inventory Management

Real-time inventory tracking, barcode integration, stock alerts, transfers, and comprehensive product management.

Customer Management

CRM with customer profiles, purchase history, loyalty programs, and activity tracking.

V

Vendor Management

Supplier database, purchase orders, payment tracking, and vendor performance analytics.

F

Financial Reporting

Comprehensive reports, profit/loss statements, tax compliance, ZATCA integration, and real-time analytics.

E

Employee Management

HR module with employee records, attendance, payroll integration, and performance tracking.

Font Family

Primary: Inter
Weights: Regular (400), Medium (500), Semibold (600), Bold (700)

Fallback: -apple-system, BlinkMacSystemFont, 'Segoe UI', Roboto, sans-serif

Usage Guidelines

• Headings: Bold/Semibold weights
• Body text: Regular weight
• Minimum size: 14px for body text
• Line height: 1.5-1.7 for readability
• Letter spacing: -0.5px for large headings

Logo Usage Guidelines

Proper logo usage ensures consistent brand recognition across all touchpoints.

BitPro GLOBAL SOFTWARE

Clear Space

Maintain a minimum clear space around the logo equal to the height of the icon. This ensures the logo remains prominent and uncluttered.

Minimum Size

Digital: 120px width minimum
Print: 1 inch (2.54cm) width minimum
Use icon-only version for smaller applications.

Backgrounds

Logo works best on white, light gray (#f8f9fa), or dark backgrounds (#1a1a1a). Ensure sufficient contrast for readability.

Color Variations

Primary logo in brand colors. White logo for dark backgrounds. Never use gradients, outlines, or other color variations.

Digital Guidelines

Standards for digital applications and online presence.

Website Design

Color Usage: Primary brand colors with white/light gray backgrounds

Typography: Inter for all text, headings in Bold/Semibold

Layout: Clean, spacious layouts with generous whitespace

Imagery: Professional business photos, avoid stock imagery clichés

Buttons: Primary actions in Deep Teal (#1e4d5f), 8-12px border radius

UI Components

Cards: White background, 16px border radius, subtle shadows

Forms: Clean inputs with 8px border radius, focus states in Sky Blue

Icons: Line style, 2px stroke weight, match brand colors

Spacing: 8px base unit, multiples of 8 for consistency

Animations: Subtle, purposeful, 200-300ms duration

Social Media

Profile Image: Icon-only logo
Cover Images: Brand gradient with key message
Post Graphics: Consistent templates using brand colors
Hashtags: #BitPro #POSSoftware #ERPSystem #BusinessManagement #AssetManagement #ZATCACompliant

Email Marketing

Header: Logo centered or left-aligned
Colors: Use brand palette consistently
CTA Buttons: Deep Teal with white text
Footer: Contact info, social links, legal text

Mobile Apps

App Icon: Icon-only version with gradient background
Splash Screen: Centered logo on white/brand color
Navigation: Bottom or side nav with icon-only logo
Gestures: Standard iOS/Android patterns

Print Guidelines

Standards for physical brand materials.

Business Collateral

Brochures: A4/Letter size, full bleed
Flyers: High-quality paper stock
Folders: Matte finish with logo on front
Color Mode: CMYK for all print materials

Stationery

Business Cards: 85mm × 55mm, 350gsm
Letterhead: A4 with header logo and footer contact
Envelopes: DL or C4 with logo
Notepads: A4/A5 with subtle header branding

Large Format

Banners: High-res logo, minimum 300 DPI
Posters: Bold headlines, key messages
Signage: Clear space guidelines strictly followed
Trade Show: Consistent brand presence
